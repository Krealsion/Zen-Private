# Zen pressure

**What it holds:** pressure Zen has met that may become work: a seam, a measured cost, a defect
with no owner yet. None of it is owed; once Zen decides it is, it moves to
[backlog.md](backlog.md).  
**Updated:** 2026-10-27, rewritten short. The old board is kept whole as
[history/closed_pressure.md](history/closed_pressure.md): each item's full story and evidence,
under the same id.  
**Beside it:** [backlog.md](backlog.md), the work owed; `zen-strategic-map.md`, the strategy.

## How this file works

- One or two lines an item: what it is, and what wakes it (the trigger that would make it work).
  Its evidence and story are in closed_pressure.md under the same id.
- States: ACT, evidence supports a bounded next step; OPEN, a defect measured and not yet taken;
  PARKED, waiting on a named trigger; SEED, a strategic candidate, not law. An item with no state
  named is WATCH: real pressure that more use should shape.
- Pressure is not authorization. A known seam in the code belongs in the repository's law, work
  decided on in backlog.md, bounded work in an issue.
- An item leaves when its fix merges or an issue takes it; its story stays in history.
- States are as last recorded, most between 2026-10-09 and 10-19. The rewrite closed only what it
  verified; that list heads closed_pressure.md.

## 9. Seeds: strategic candidates, not current work

These shape completeness tests only; each needs its own consumer and an authorized phase first.

- **Flow as projection:** facts, values, operators, requests, effects and authority as a maker's
  composition language; a graphical editor is one projection over it, not its only form.
- **Metadata as inspectable evidence:** identity and provenance facts gather under clear
  claimants, and never become authority over behaviour.
- **Generated ceremony:** provider, schema, ABI and build glue may be generated; the C++ compiler
  parses C++, and Zen builds no parser for arbitrary C++.
- **Two forms bridged by Flow** (`goals/two-forms.md`): a live interpreted form and a generated
  durable C++ form, Flow reading code through the Loom ABI's handles. "A raw node is an operator
  iff it has no bus access, transitively" is the founder's hypothesis, not law.
- Info rebuilt as a one-slot inspector left the seeds: the founder ordered it as BL-WORK-10.
