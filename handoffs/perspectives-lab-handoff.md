# Handoff: the Perspectives Lab, again -- a weaver's day, drafted in the terminal window

From the founder (Krealsion), 2026-10-23. Written by Vision for a fresh session; it replaces this
page's version of 2026-10-19. Nothing below replaces reading the source.

## 1. The role

- **What you make:** perspectives. A perspective is a desk arranged for one task a weaver does in a
  day: which panes, where, with which keys, and how the weaver enters it, leaves it and comes back
  to it. The founder's notes (theme 6 in `Zen/private/backlog.md`, section 1b): "a workspace
  arranges tools and material; a perspective presents them for a task and may span workspaces".
- **The tasks:** the eight in `Zen/rebuild-scaffold/perspectives/README.md` -- Flow coding, editor
  coding, git, UI work, exploration, triage, pairing, tailoring -- and others a day turns out to
  hold.
- **What changed** (the founder, 2026-10-23): the last pages ran to 30 KB each and asked him to
  picture desks from text, so he could not decide them without living them. Now:
  1. **Drafted in the terminal window first.** Each desk is drawn in cells, at a terminal size you
     state; then what the OS window adds, described rather than drawn. The terminal draft is the
     floor a perspective keeps as the two windows grow apart; the OS window is never held to it.
  2. **Drawings only.** Nothing run, built or loaded, and no setup file: a perspective the founder
     takes becomes a setup through a lane.
  3. **Alternatives with a default.** Each perspective, and each choice in it, comes with its
     alternatives and the default you pick, with why. Your defaults are provisional, marked as
     yours, until the founder has used them; an alternative not picked stays as an option.
  4. **The day as a whole.** A page of its own on how a weaver moves between perspectives through a
     day -- which panes and keys they share, where they fight -- and your quality analysis of the
     set as one workflow, improved round by round, back and forth across the domains.
  5. **Short pages.** One task a page, about 120 lines at most: read in five minutes.
  6. **Every reference resolves** (section 4).

## 4. The check: every reference resolves

- Every name you write in backticks -- a file, a pane, a shape, a key, an action id, a setting, a
  law -- is copied from the line you cite, at the commit you name, or marked `proposed`. Nothing is
  written from memory or by analogy: a name that looks right is the error this check is for.
- Run the check on every page before each commit. A tool of your own for it lives in your folder.
- `Zen/stranger/glossary.md`, once it exists, is a stranger's glossary checked against the source:
  a place to start for terms, checked as you check your own.

## 5. What a page holds

- the task, and its moments through a day, in order;
- the desk in the terminal window, drawn in ASCII at the size you state, with its keys, and how the
  weaver enters it, leaves it and returns;
- what the OS window adds, in a short list;
- what `main` offers today and what is missing; a missing tool drawn as a weave -- an office or a
  pane -- that talks only by messages, its shapes named by the naming rule: a request ends
  `Requested`, an answer `Answered` or `Refused`, a deed in the past tense, an observation a noun;
- the alternatives, and the default you picked, with why;
- at most three questions for the founder, each with your default.

Mark what is measured, what is by reading and what is design.

## 6. What not to do

- No running, building or loading anything, and no setup file.
- No prompt for an executor, and no pull request, commit, push, branch or issue in Zengine or Loom.
- Nothing written outside `Zen/rebuild-scaffold/perspectives/`. The scaffold's other pages are
  drafts the founder keeps: read them, never edit them.
- No design of the desk owner's storage or messages, of the settings pane, or of anything G2 holds.
- No credential. A perspective that would need one (pushing to a remote, say) says where and stops.
- A security or capability gap is written in Zen alone, never in a public place.
- A Zen commit is authored and committed as Krealsion <krealsion@gmail.com>, with no AI co-author
  line and no AI attribution in any form. Commit only your folder: other sessions commit to Zen
  too.
- Lines of 100 columns at most, in ASCII.

## 7. Working with the founder

- Lists over prose; the simplest reading first; one question per message.
- Define a new term where it first appears, and show a shape's fields when you propose one.
- Peer-level honesty: say what you do not know, and where the code disagrees with a plan.
- When you write a file, give him its path, not its contents.
