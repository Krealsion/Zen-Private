# 2026-11

## The plan rebuilt (2026-11-16)

**Wanted.** The founder's boot drive failed on 2026-11-15, and with the machine down the plan was
read whole for the first time in weeks. The strategic map had gone four weeks stale while the
direction moved, and two pieces of work (the WASM tier's last stages, the Linux sandbox's
retirement) had fallen off every list without a word. He asked for `private/` rebuilt from the
ground up, the old work kept but safe to forget, so that many goals stay in view at once.

**Built.** The roadmap became the file changed most: prose of where Zen stands and where it goes,
the directions in order, the lanes and the calls no goal owns. Each goal became a checklist of
its own in `goals/` (29 of them, both lost pieces among them), which shrinks as work lands. The
backlog keeps only work decided and not pursued, each item with what brings it back; pressure
kept its items under a new preamble, the seven a goal took moved into it. The old plan moved
whole into `archive/2026-11-15/` (52 files and a copy of pressure), and the 920 links that left
it were mended to resolve from there (`Zen/pass-tools/private-archive/mend_links.py`).

**Taught.** A goal leaves the plan only by a stated end -- finished, paused, parked, or dropped on
the founder's word -- and the roadmap changes the same day the direction does.

**Records.** The Vision constitution and the kickstart, in the Project (`claude/`); this
repository's commits `e51ec9d` (the move) and `9376276` (the links).

## The session key and the schema id (2026-11-10)

**Wanted.** The outside audit of 2026-11-09 found two defects in Loom, both kept private until
fixed: a session key the host could not make owner-only (on FAT or exFAT) was used anyway, and a
schema's 64-bit id alone decided whether two weaves agreed on its shape.

**Built.** The key is refused where the host cannot make it owner-only (Loom `e48b720`), and a
schema's structure is compared wherever its id decides agreement, option A (`d8d2b82`): both
merged from their advisories' forks on 2026-11-10, and both advisories published that day
(GHSA-rwq8-7q73-2wpf, GHSA-rh2c-xrxm-m4rq). The catch the audit asked of `loom-host` landed as
Loom #20 (2026-11-12), so its issue was never needed.

**Taught.** The plan carried the goal as open for nine days after it finished, because it was
installed at the rebuild without its day-one check. A goal moved into a new plan is checked
against `main` the day it moves.

**Records.** `reportbacks/private-security-pair-RB.md`; the audit
(`reportbacks/zen-audit-2026-11-09.md`) and its recheck (`reportbacks/audit-recheck-RB.md`);
the goal's checklist, `archive/goals/security-pair.md`.
